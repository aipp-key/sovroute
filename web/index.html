Why SovRoute

Non-custodial by design
Funds never sit in an operator pool or shared hot wallet. Cryptographic hold invoices ensure your sovereignty.
Deterministic recovery model
Onchain timelocks enforce automatic refund paths. If counterparty drops offline, your assets are guaranteed to return.
Lightning to Base execution
Sub-second Bitcoin Lightning payment resolves into immediate USDC delivery on Base L2 via HTLC atomic swap.
Self-hosted first
Zero external trust dependencies. Deploy SovRoute on your own node alongside Bitcoin Core, LND, and Base RPC.

How it works

01
Fund Hold Invoice
Payer locks Bitcoin satoshis into an HTLC hold invoice tied to a secure hash.
02
Lock Base USDC
Operator escrows matching USDC on Base smart contract with the identical hash.
03
Reveal Preimage
USDC claim on Base reveals the secret preimage to the public blockchain.
04
Instant Settlement
Lightning invoice captures the preimage and settles atomically. Zero counterparty risk.

🛡️ If claim does not occur before timelock expiry, the refund path is mathematically guaranteed.
